# Risk Register

## Overview

This project develops a risk register for a financial institution by identifying critical assets, evaluating potential threats, estimating business impact, and prioritizing risks based on likelihood and severity.

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Risk Assessment
- Asset Management
- Threat Analysis
- Risk Prioritization
- Security Planning

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Risk Matrix

---

## Frameworks / Concepts

- Risk Management
- Risk Assessment Methodology

---

## Project Description

This project documents organizational risks affecting a financial institution by evaluating critical assets, associated threats, business impact, and mitigation priorities.

---

## Objectives

- Identify organizational assets.
- Evaluate security risks.
- Prioritize remediation efforts.
- Support security decision-making.

---

## Methodology

Each identified risk was assessed according to its likelihood of occurrence and potential business impact. Risk priority was calculated using a standardized risk matrix to determine remediation priorities.

---

## Results

Multiple operational, technical, and physical risks were identified and prioritized. The completed risk register provides a structured approach for allocating security resources effectively.

---

## Key Takeaways

Maintaining an updated risk register enables organizations to proactively manage security risks and prioritize investments based on measurable business impact.
